International School Dhaka
Brand identity · March 2025
Case study / 01

A consistent identity.
A connected community.

The ISD Brand Identity Guidelines (March 2025) describe a unified visual system for the school’s communications: logo and clear space, colour, mascot and seal, typography, photography, co-branding, merchandise, stationery, corporate brochures and digital presentations.

My contribution

As Creative Media Specialist, I played a key role in shaping and applying the visual direction, translating the school’s identity into practical communication materials and supporting consistent use across channels.

Visual system

Worked with a recognisable system built around the ISD logo, clear-space rules, approved colour palette, Montserrat typography and consistent photography standards.

Brand applications

Applied the identity across promotional graphics, photography-led campaigns, publication layouts, digital content and school communications.
